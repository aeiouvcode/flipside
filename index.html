Vault + privacy

          

            
Right now nothing is stored when you close this tab. Set a passphrase and Flipside keeps your data on this device only, encrypted with AES-GCM - the same class of encryption banks use. The passphrase never leaves your browser, and there is no account to hack because there is no server.

            

              
              
            

            

            
Forget the passphrase and the data is unrecoverable - that is the point of real encryption. Write it down somewhere safe.

          

          

            
Your data is encrypted on this device with your passphrase. Flipside auto-locks after 10 minutes away from the page.
